# itos-cc stays AGPL-3.0 under the MIT template

## Context and Problem Statement

Adopting go-template-itos (MIT) with --merge leaves LICENSE in conflict with itos-cc's AGPL-3.0. Which license does itos-cc carry after adoption? Options: keep AGPL-3.0, or switch to MIT.

Asked as q-47, about template-adoption.

## Considered Options

The options are those the question names.

## Decision Outcome

Keep AGPL-3.0, 2026-10-11: the adoption resolves LICENSE to itos-cc's.

### Consequences

Adopting or updating go-template-itos resolves LICENSE and CONTRIBUTORS.md to itos-cc's AGPL-3.0; the template's MIT text and its authorization sentence never land here.
